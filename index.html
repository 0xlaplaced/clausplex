FEW KEYS. PLENTY TO THINK ABOUT.
A few rules for the road.
1. Make a path
Use the arrow keys to move and clear the circuit tiles. Push rocks sideways when there is room. They fall, so be careful underneath.
2. Find the sparks
Bring the spark counter to zero, then step into the exit. Hold Space and a direction to collect an adjacent object without moving. After a win, choose Next level to continue.
3. Read the room
Arrow ports let you pass in the marked directions. Some change gravity. Terminals detonate yellow disks. Collect red disks, then hold Space to place one.
4. Take your time
Press P to pause and R to restart. On touchscreens, use the direction pad and hold GRAB with a direction. Leaving the page ends the current attempt.
Part I runs the first 37 original OpenSupaplex levels unchanged. Movement, falling objects and puzzle solutions follow the original rules.


A SMALL REIMAGINING
From Supaplex to Clausplex.
An independent community game inspired by the world of Claus. This is not an official Claus game.
Engine: Sergio Padrino and OpenSupaplex contributors (GPLv3). Supaplex: Philip Jespersen and Michael Stopp. The first 37 original levels are preserved in Part I. Claus uses the brand’s own character asset; terrain artwork and interface were made for this adaptation.
